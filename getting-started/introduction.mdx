## What is Lawdie?

Lawdie is a legal operating system for modern law firms. It brings together AI-powered research, document drafting, email management, client intake, call center tools, and practice integrations into one platform — so attorneys spend less time on overhead and more time on client work.

Every part of Lawdie is purpose-built for legal professionals. The AI understands legal language, cites real cases with Bluebook citations, drafts documents that meet jurisdictional standards, and works alongside the tools your firm already uses.

## Who it's for

Lawdie is built for attorneys and the teams that support them:

- **Litigation attorneys** — case law research, RERA brief drafting, motion generation, and document analysis
- **IP attorneys** — patent and trademark searches, prior art landscapes, and prosecution document drafting
- **General practice firms** — client intake, referral management, contract drafting, and call logging
- **Legal operations teams** — workflow standardization through templates, matter organization, and cross-tool integrations
