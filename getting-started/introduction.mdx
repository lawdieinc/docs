## What is Lawdie?

Lawdie is an AI-powered legal assistant platform built for attorneys and legal teams. It combines a multi-agent AI system with deep integrations into the tools lawyers already use — Gmail, Google Calendar, Microsoft OneDrive, Clio, and more.

Instead of switching between tabs, Lawdie routes your requests to specialized AI agents that handle everything from case law research to patent searches to email drafts — all from a single chat interface.

## Who is it for?

Lawdie is designed for:

- **Litigation attorneys** who need fast case law research and brief-writing support
- **IP attorneys** working with patent and trademark searches
- **General practice firms** managing client intake, referrals, and document workflows
- **Legal operations teams** coordinating across email, calendar, and case management systems

## How it works

Every message you send goes through a supervisor agent that reads your practice area and routes the request to the most appropriate specialized agent.

<Steps>
  <Step title="You send a message">
    Type a question or request in the chat — attach documents if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supervisor routes the request">
    The supervisor agent identifies your intent and selects the right specialist agent based on your practice area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agent executes and streams back">
    The selected agent calls the necessary tools (case law databases, USPTO, Gmail, etc.) and streams the response back to you in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
