## Your first workflow — research a case and send an email

Walk through this end-to-end example to see Kiwi AI's capabilities working together.

### Step 1 — Open Kiwi AI

Log in to Lawdie and navigate to **Kiwi AI** from the left sidebar. This is your main interface for all AI-assisted legal work.

### Step 2 — Connect Gmail

Before sending email, you need to authorize your Gmail account.

1. Click the **Integrations** icon in the Kiwi AI toolbar (or go to **Settings → Integrations**)
2. Select **Connect Google**
3. Complete the OAuth flow — grant Gmail send and read permissions when prompted
4. You'll be returned to the chat with a confirmation that Gmail is connected

### Step 3 — Ask Kiwi AI to research, draft, and send

Try this prompt in the chat:

```
Research the top cases on breach of fiduciary duty in Delaware courts,
then draft a professional email to opposing@lawfirm.com summarizing
the strongest precedents for my client's position, and send it.
```

Kiwi AI runs the full workflow automatically:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Legal Research searches case law">
    Queries CourtListener for breach of fiduciary duty cases in Delaware, retrieving the most relevant opinions and citations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rule Statement extracts the key rules">
    Pulls the operative legal standard from each case and scores them by how well they fit the argument.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email drafts the message">
    Composes a professional email summarizing the strongest precedents, with inline citations, addressed to the recipient you specified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email sends it">
    Sends through your connected Gmail account and confirms delivery in the chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Add *"show me the draft before sending"* to your prompt if you want to review the email before it goes out.
</Note>
