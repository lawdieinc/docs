## Overview

Email Management is a dedicated inbox dashboard inside Lawdie. Connect your Gmail or Microsoft Outlook account and work through your emails without switching apps — every email comes with an AI summary, a signal on whether it needs a response, and a one-click AI reply generator.

## AI email summaries

Every email in the dashboard shows a one-line AI summary so you can triage at a glance without opening each message.

For longer threads, click into the email to see a full thread summary — a 2–4 sentence recap of the entire conversation, including who said what and what was agreed.

## Response detection

Each email is automatically flagged with a response status:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Response required** | The email contains a direct question, request, or action item addressed to you |
| **FYI** | Informational — no reply expected |
| **Awaiting your reply** | You haven't responded to a thread that needs one |
| **No action needed** | Newsletters, notifications, and automated messages |

This lets you quickly filter to only the emails that need your attention.

## AI reply generation

For any email that requires a response, click **Generate Reply** to have Lawdie draft a reply for you.

The generated reply:
- Matches the tone and formality of the original email
- Addresses each specific question or request in the thread
- Uses your name and signature automatically
- Can be edited before sending

To send without edits, click **Send**. To review and adjust, click into the draft and edit inline before sending.

## Filtering and search

| Filter | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Response required** | Show only emails flagged as needing a reply |
| **Unread** | Show only unread emails |
| **By sender** | Filter by a specific person or domain |
| **By matter** | If you've linked a matter, show emails associated with it |
| **Date range** | Narrow to a specific time window |

Use the search bar to find any email by keyword, sender, or subject across your connected inbox.

## Linking emails to matters

Any email can be linked to a [Matter](/agents/matters) in Kiwi AI. Once linked, the email thread appears as context inside that matter's conversation — so when you ask Kiwi AI a question about the case, it has visibility into the relevant email history.

To link an email, open it in the dashboard and click **Add to Matter**, then select the matter from the dropdown.
