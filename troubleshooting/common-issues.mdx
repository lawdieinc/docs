## The response stopped halfway through

Kiwi started responding but the text cut off and the loading indicator kept spinning.

**Try these in order:**
1. Refresh the page — the connection occasionally drops on network changes
2. If it happens consistently on the same question, try rephrasing or breaking it into a shorter query
3. If it keeps happening, contact **support@lawdie.co**

---

## I'm getting wrong or irrelevant research results

The cases or statutes Kiwi returned don't match what you were looking for.

**Try being more specific in your prompt:**
- Include the jurisdiction: *"9th Circuit"*, *"New York state courts"*, *"SDNY"*
- Name the exact legal element: instead of *"negligence"*, say *"duty to warn under strict products liability"*
- Give your client's facts — the agent uses them to score how relevant each case is

If you're researching a specific statute, cite it directly: *"Look up 42 U.S.C. § 1983"* returns the text of the statute without a broad search.

## Kiwi went to the wrong specialist

You asked a patent question but got a general research response, or a drafting request turned into a research session.

**How routing works:** Kiwi reads intent from your first sentence. Be explicit about what you want:
- *"USPTO patent search for..."* → routes to Patent agent
- *"Draft a motion to dismiss for..."* → routes to Document Drafting agent
- *"Rule statements and RE/RA for..."* → routes to Rule Statement agent

If your account is set up for a specific practice area and routing is consistently wrong, contact support to update your profile.

## A document I uploaded isn't being read correctly

Kiwi returned a summary that doesn't match the document, or said it couldn't read the file.

**Check the following:**
- Supported formats are **PDF** and **DOCX** — scanned image PDFs without text layers cannot be read
- Files must be under **50MB**
- If the PDF is a scanned document, try running it through OCR first before uploading

## My Google (Gmail / Calendar) connection stopped working

The email or calendar agent says it's not connected or returns an authorization error.

**Fix:**
1. Go to **Settings → Integrations**
2. Click **Reconnect** next to Google
3. Complete the sign-in and approve all requested permissions
4. If you use a Google Workspace account and the button doesn't work, your IT admin may need to allow third-party app access

---

## My Clio integration isn't syncing documents

Documents drafted in Lawdie aren't showing up in the right Clio matter.

**Fix:**
1. Go to **Settings → Integrations** and reconnect Clio — the session token may have expired
2. When asking Kiwi to save to Clio, use the **exact matter name** as it appears in Clio (including capitalization)
3. If the matter still doesn't sync, try opening the matter in Clio first, then re-running the request in Lawdie

---

## I can't log in

Forgot your password, or the login page isn't loading.

- Use **Forgot Password** on the login screen to reset via email
- If you signed up with Google, click **Continue with Google** instead of entering a password
- For firm-managed accounts, contact your workspace admin — they control access and seat assignments

---

## A case citation link isn't working

You clicked a Bluebook citation hyperlink in a response and got a 404 or empty page.

CourtListener and OpenLaws occasionally restructure URLs. The case itself is almost certainly still accessible — try:
1. Copying the citation text (e.g. *555 U.S. 7*) and searching it directly on [courtlistener.com](https://www.courtlistener.com)
2. Asking Kiwi: *"Find the full text of [case name]"* to re-fetch it
