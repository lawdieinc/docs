## What it does

The patent search agent queries the USPTO patent database to find existing patents, prior art, and pending applications. It's designed for IP attorneys conducting freedom-to-operate analyses, patentability searches, and litigation research.

## Example prompts

- *"Search for patents related to noise-canceling headphone technology filed after 2018."*
- *"Find prior art for a machine learning model that predicts stock prices using sentiment analysis."*
- *"Look up patent US10,234,567 and summarize its claims."*
- *"Is there existing IP around automated document classification using transformer models?"*

## Tips for better results

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Use technical keywords, not brand names">
    Search by the underlying technology (e.g., "convolutional neural network image recognition") rather than product names, which may not appear in patent filings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Specify a date range">
    Narrow to a filing window to find the most relevant prior art for your patentability analysis.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ask for claim summaries">
    Patent claim language is dense. Ask Lawdie to summarize the independent claims in plain English.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
