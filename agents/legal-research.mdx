## What it does

The legal research agent searches federal and state court databases to find relevant case law, pull docket entries, and retrieve full opinion text. It's the starting point for any research-heavy task.

## What it searches

| Source | Coverage |
|--------|----------|
| **CourtListener** | Federal and state court opinions with full opinion text |
| **OpenLaws** | State case law, statutes, regulations, and court rules |
| **PACER** | Federal court dockets and filings |

Every case returned is automatically checked to confirm it's still good law before it appears in your response. If a case has been overruled, Lawdie flags it rather than silently citing bad authority.

## Example prompts

- *"Find cases on qualified immunity for police officers in the 11th Circuit from the last 5 years."*
- *"Pull the docket for case 1:23-cv-01234 in the Southern District of New York."*
- *"What did the Supreme Court say about personal jurisdiction in Ford Motor Co. v. Montana?"*

## How to get the best results

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Specify the jurisdiction">
    Narrow results significantly by naming the circuit, district, or state court. Without a jurisdiction, the search runs across all courts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Include the legal issue">
    Frame your query around the specific legal element you're researching (e.g., "failure to state a claim under 12(b)(6)") rather than the case name alone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ask for opinion text on key cases">
    If a case looks relevant, ask Lawdie to pull the full opinion text. This feeds into the Rule Statement agent for RERA drafting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Connecting to RERA drafting

Legal research pairs directly with the [Rule Statement & RERA agent](/agents/rule-statement-rera). After finding relevant cases, ask Lawdie to extract rule statements and draft RE/RA paragraphs for your brief.

```
"Find the top cases on breach of fiduciary duty in Delaware, then draft RERA paragraphs for my motion."
```

Lawdie will run the full workflow: search → extract rule statements → generate RERA.
