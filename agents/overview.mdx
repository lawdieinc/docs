## What is Kiwi AI?

Kiwi AI is Lawdie's intelligent chat assistant built specifically for legal professionals. It routes your requests to specialized AI capabilities — legal research, patent searches, email drafting, document generation, and more — all from a single conversation interface.

You don't select an agent or configure a pipeline. Just describe what you need in plain language and Kiwi AI handles the rest.

## Jurisdiction selection

Set your jurisdiction before starting a research or drafting session so Kiwi AI tailors its results and legal standards to the right court system.

- Click the **Jurisdiction** selector in the Kiwi AI toolbar
- Choose from federal circuits, state courts, or a specific district
- The selection persists for the duration of your session and can be changed at any point

When a jurisdiction is set, legal research queries are scoped to that court system by default, and drafted documents reference the correct local rules and procedural requirements.

## Content filters

Content filters let you control the scope and tone of Kiwi AI's output.

| Filter | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Safe search** | Excludes graphic case details from research results |
| **Formality** | Sets the writing style for drafts — formal (briefs, motions) or plain (client memos, emails) |
| **Source restrictions** | Limit research to specific databases (e.g., federal courts only, state courts only) |
| **Citation format** | Choose between Bluebook, ALWD, or jurisdiction-specific citation styles |

Access filters from the **Settings** icon in the Kiwi AI toolbar.

## Prompt Vault

Prompt Vault lets you save, organize, and reuse prompts you use frequently.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save a prompt">
    After typing a prompt in the chat, click the **bookmark icon** next to the input field to save it to your vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and tag it">
    Give the prompt a name and optional tags (e.g., "12(b)(6) research", "demand letter", "NDA draft") so you can find it quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reuse it">
    Click the **Vault** icon in the toolbar to browse saved prompts. Select one to load it into the chat input, where you can edit it before sending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Prompt Vault is shared across all your chat sessions — prompts saved in one conversation are available in all others.
