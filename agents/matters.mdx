## What are Matters?

Matters are how Kiwi AI organizes your work. Each matter corresponds to a client case or project. When you work inside a matter, Kiwi AI automatically scopes its context to the relevant details — client name, case number, jurisdiction, and any documents you've associated with that matter.

This means you can pick up a conversation mid-case without re-explaining the background every time.

## Creating a matter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Matters panel">
    Click **Matters** in the Kiwi AI sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Matter">
    Enter the matter name, client name, case number (optional), and jurisdiction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add documents">
    Upload any relevant files — complaints, contracts, discovery materials — directly to the matter. Kiwi AI will reference these when you ask questions inside the matter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a conversation">
    Open a chat session within the matter. Kiwi AI carries the matter context automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Matter context in conversations

When you're working inside a matter, Kiwi AI knows:

- The client name and opposing party
- The case number and jurisdiction
- Documents attached to the matter
- Your conversation history within that matter

You can reference these naturally in your prompts:

- *"Draft a demand letter to opposing counsel using the facts from the complaint."*
- *"Search for cases that support our position on the damages issue."*
- *"What deadlines are coming up based on the scheduling order I uploaded?"*

## Organizing matters

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Tags** | Label matters by practice area, status (active, closed), or client type |
| **Search** | Find any matter by name, case number, or client |
| **Archive** | Close out completed matters without deleting them — archived matters remain searchable |
| **Share** | Share a matter with co-counsel or other team members within your workspace |

## Linking to Clio

If you've connected the [Clio integration](/integrations/clio), matters in Lawdie can be linked to Clio matters. This syncs generated documents, call logs, and time entries back to your practice management system automatically.
