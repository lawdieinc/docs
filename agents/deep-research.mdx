## What it does

Deep Research runs an extended, multi-step investigation on a legal question. Unlike a single agent query, Deep Research autonomously plans a research strategy, queries multiple sources in sequence, cross-references findings, and delivers a structured report with citations.

Use it when a question requires more than a single search — for example, tracing how a legal standard has evolved across circuits, building a comprehensive prior art landscape, or researching an unfamiliar area of law from scratch.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="You describe the research question">
    Provide a question or topic in plain language. The more context you give — jurisdiction, time range, specific legal issue — the more targeted the output.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kiwi AI plans the research strategy">
    Deep Research breaks the question into sub-queries and determines which sources to hit: CourtListener, USPTO, web sources, regulatory databases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Multi-source queries run in parallel">
    Searches execute across sources simultaneously. Kiwi AI retrieves opinions, regulatory guidance, secondary sources, and news coverage relevant to the question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Findings are cross-referenced and synthesized">
    Results are compared, conflicts are flagged, and the strongest authorities are ranked. Redundant or weak sources are filtered out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A structured report is delivered">
    You receive a formatted report with an executive summary, key findings, relevant authorities with citations, and identified gaps or open questions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example prompts

- *"Give me a deep research report on how courts in the 9th and 11th Circuits have treated the economic loss rule in software contract disputes over the last 10 years."*
- *"Research the current regulatory landscape for AI-generated legal documents — federal guidance, state bar opinions, and any pending legislation."*
- *"Build a prior art landscape for natural language processing applied to contract review, covering patents, academic sources, and commercial products."*
- *"How have courts treated non-compete agreements post-FTC rule? I need a full circuit-by-circuit breakdown."*

## Output format

Deep Research reports are delivered as structured documents with:

- **Executive summary** — 3–5 sentence overview of the key findings
- **Key authorities** — ranked list of the most relevant cases, statutes, and regulatory guidance with Bluebook citations
- **Detailed findings** — section-by-section analysis of the research question
- **Circuit or jurisdiction breakdown** — where applicable, how different courts or regulators have treated the issue
- **Open questions** — areas where the law is unsettled or evolving
- **Sources** — full list of all sources consulted

Reports can be downloaded as DOCX for further editing.

## Research depth settings

Control how thorough Deep Research goes via the depth setting in the toolbar before starting:

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Standard** | 3–5 minutes, broad coverage of the main authorities |
| **Thorough** | 10–15 minutes, full circuit analysis and secondary sources |
| **Exhaustive** | 20+ minutes, comprehensive landscape including regulatory history and academic commentary |
