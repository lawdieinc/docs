## What it does

The document drafting agent generates legal documents based on your instructions. Provide the key details — parties, facts, jurisdiction, and intent — and it produces a structured DOCX file ready for your review and edits.

## Document types

- Demand letters
- Settlement agreements
- Contracts and agreements
- Motions and briefs
- Cease and desist letters
- Client intake forms
- Referral agreements
- Legal memos

## Example prompts

- *"Draft a demand letter to ABC Corp for unpaid invoices totaling $45,000. My client is XYZ LLC, jurisdiction is California."*
- *"Generate a standard NDA between two parties for a software development project."*
- *"Write a motion to dismiss under Rule 12(b)(6) based on these facts: [facts]."*
- *"Create a referral agreement between my firm and co-counsel for a 30% referral fee."*

## How drafts are delivered

Generated documents are returned as downloadable DOCX files directly in the chat. You can download, edit in Word, and finalize from there.

<Note>
  For documents requiring case law support (e.g., a motion), pair the document drafting agent with the [Legal Research](/agents/legal-research) and [Rule Statement & RERA](/agents/rule-statement-rera) agents first to build the legal argument, then ask Lawdie to draft the motion.
</Note>

## Tips

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Provide complete party information">
    Include full legal names, addresses, and roles (plaintiff/defendant, licensor/licensee) for cleaner output that requires less manual editing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Specify jurisdiction">
    State and federal jurisdiction affects required language, statute references, and formatting conventions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Attach reference documents">
    Upload an existing contract or template and ask Lawdie to adapt it — this produces faster, more accurate drafts than starting from scratch.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
