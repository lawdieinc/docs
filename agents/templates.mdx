## What are Templates?

Templates are pre-built prompts that standardize repeatable tasks. Instead of typing the same instructions every time you draft a demand letter or run a conflict check, you create a template once and reuse it with a single click — filling in only the case-specific details each time.

Templates are different from [Prompt Vault](/agents/overview#prompt-vault) entries: templates are structured with variable placeholders, designed to be shared across a team, and tied to specific workflows.

## Creating a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Templates library">
    Click **Templates** in the Kiwi AI sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Template">
    Give the template a name and description, and choose a category (Research, Drafting, Email, Analysis).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the prompt with variables">
    Use double curly braces for fields that change each time:

    ```
    Draft a demand letter to {{opposing_party}} on behalf of {{client_name}}.
    Jurisdiction: {{jurisdiction}}.
    Claims: {{claims}}.
    Damages: {{damages}}.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and share">
    Save the template to your personal library or publish it to your team workspace so everyone can use it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using a template

1. Click **Templates** in the toolbar or type `/template` in the chat
2. Search or browse to find the template you want
3. Fill in the variable fields in the form that appears
4. Click **Run** — Kiwi AI executes the prompt with your inputs

## Built-in templates

Lawdie includes a set of starter templates for common legal tasks:

| Template | Category | What it does |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Demand Letter | Drafting | Drafts a formal demand letter with claims and damages |
| Case Law Research Brief | Research | Searches for relevant cases and summarizes the top results |
| RERA Paragraph | Drafting | Runs the full rule statement → RERA workflow for a single argument |
| Conflict Check | Research | Searches for any potential conflicts of interest for a new client or matter |
| NDA Review | Analysis | Reviews an NDA and flags unusual or missing provisions |
| Client Intake Summary | Analysis | Extracts key facts from an intake form or document |
| Motion to Dismiss Outline | Drafting | Generates a 12(b)(6) motion outline based on provided facts |

## Team templates

Templates marked as **Team** are available to everyone in your workspace. Workspace admins can publish, edit, or archive team templates from the Templates library.

Use team templates to enforce consistency across your firm — standard formatting, required jurisdictional language, and approved clause sets.
